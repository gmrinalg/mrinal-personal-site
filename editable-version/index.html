hello, I'm Mrinal

This work began with my own social anxiety. I'm still meeting it, and it keeps teaching me.

I'm an Experiential Learning & Human Connection Facilitator, working where spontaneous expression, play, emotional intelligence and inner transformation meet.

I trained as a designer at the National Institute of Design, and I bring design thinking together with psychology and theatre to build spaces where deep, safe inner exploration can happen. I've facilitated intensive and short-format workshops with hundreds of people, both in person and online.

With playfulness, honesty and care, I help people meet hidden hurts, understand their inner parts, and move toward more freedom, safe connection and flow. Much of my work is with shame: how we can learn to accept ourselves and build a supportive, loving inner system for ourselves and for each other.

what a session feels like

Slow at first. Then surprisingly alive.

  1. 1

    Arrive

    We settle in, check in, and agree on what makes this space safe. You always get to say no.

  2. 2

    Warm up

    Gentle play, movement and a little silliness to wake the body up and quiet the judge.

  3. 3

    Explore

    Improv, Meisner, clown and rasa work help us meet hidden hurts and the inner parts behind them.

  4. 4

    Integrate

    We reflect with care and find small, real ways to carry the freedom back into your life.
